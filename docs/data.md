# Data sources, variables and limitations

All raw files are listed with URL, retrieval date, SHA-256 checksum and licence in
`data/MANIFEST.yaml` (generated by `scripts/fetch_data.py`). Transcribed values with
page and table citations are in `data/transcribed/entry_a.csv` (long) and
`entry_b.csv` (wide, typed independently); `tests/test_double_entry.py` reconciles them
and compares the Federal Register tables with values parsed by code from the XML.

| Source | Files | Variables used | Limitations |
|---|---|---|---|
| Proposed rule, 91 FR 65699 (FR Doc. 2026-21155) | `federal_register/2026-21155.{xml,pdf}` | Tables IV-1, V-1, VI-1; 2025 exemption totals; deadlines; comment solicitations | Numbers from the PDF were checked against the XML extraction |
| Set 2 final rule, 91 FR 16388 (FR Doc. 2026-06275) | `federal_register/2026-06275.{xml,pdf}` | Equations (80.1405(c), p. 16488, image); Tables IV.C-1 to V.D-1 (images, pp. 16439–16441); carryover discussion | Table images transcribed by hand; one ERVO cell needs unpublished precision (2024 advanced) |
| Docket memoranda 0789, 0790, 0956, 0961, 0954 | `regulations_gov/` | Inputs G, D, RG, RD, GE, DE, RFV, SRERV; RIN prices; fuel-cost appendices | Printed from spreadsheets to two decimals; the spreadsheets themselves are not in the docket |
| EPA SRE decision documents (EPA-420-R-25-010, -25-013, -26-004, -26-017) | `epa/` | Petition-based exempted RVO by compliance year, counts, decision dates | Aggregated; refinery-level appendices confidential |
| EPA RFS dashboard, SRE sheet (Tables SRE-1 to SRE-3) | `epa_dashboard/sre_table*.csv` | Reported and estimated exempted volumes and RVOs by year; petition decisions; per-petition decision dates since July 2022 | Pre-2019 volumes are estimates; per-petition dates only from July 2022 |
| EPA RFS dashboard, RVO sheet (Tables 1, 2, 5, 6) | `epa_dashboard/rvo_table*` | Projected versus reported gasoline-plus-diesel volumes; projected and reported obligations; RINs retired; deficits | Projections for 2020 are the revised (2022) values |
| Set 2 RIA (EPA-420-R-26-011) | `epa/420r26011.pdf` | Carryover RIN tables 1.8.1-1 to 1.8.3-1; Table 10.5.4-1 | — |
| Past annual rules (2011–2025) | `federal_register/*.xml` | "Values for Terms" input tables; publication dates | Parsed by code and transcribed by hand |
| eCFR 40 CFR 80.1405, 80.1427, 80.1451 (as of 2026-11-20) | `ecfr/` | Standards table; carryover cap; deficit rule; reporting deadlines | Equations are images in eCFR |
| EIA product supplied (MGFUPUS2, MDIUPUS2) | `eia/*.xls` | Context for fuel-use projections | Not the RFS obligated volume; EPA's dashboard is used for errors |
| Crossref metadata | `crossref/*.json` | Reference checking | — |
| farmdoc daily 16:204 (Hubbs and Irwin, 2026) | fetched on demand | Independent cross-check of derived quantities | Secondary source (rank 5); not used for parameters |

Two hosts, `downloads.regulations.gov` and `edap.epa.gov`, return HTTP 403 to
non-browser clients; the files from those hosts were retrieved through an interactive
browser session and their checksums are recorded and verified.

## Known discrepancies in the published record

* Set 2 Table IV.C-1 lists 2024 exempted gasoline 4.50 and diesel 3.29 (sum 7.79) with a
  total of 7.78; the 2024 advanced-biofuel ERVO (300 million RINs) requires an unrounded
  total of at least 7.7836 billion gallons. All other cells reproduce from the printed values.
* The proposal describes the Set 2 rounding rule as rounding volumes "ending in five"
  down for 2026 and up for 2027; only a floor (2026) and ceiling (2027) to 10 million
  RINs reproduces every published Set 2 reallocation volume.
* The dashboard reports a 2025 exempted RVO of 1,750 million RINs (13,326 million
  gallons) against the petition-based 1,760 million (13,380 million gallons) in the
  proposal; two 2025 petitions were pending as of the export date.
